The bank intentionally avoids client facts, clinical advice, religious instruction, political
messages, promises about outcomes, and language that glorifies overwork. Its themes are dignity,
careful attention, trustworthy records, humility, sustainable boundaries, steady work, humane
systems, visible uncertainty, collaboration, and practical hope.
