This pack contains fourteen feature prompts from the overall Sati gap review and five related operations/integration prompts. Each numbered section is self-contained: copy the instructions under its heading into the coding task you want to start. Writing this pack does not start implementation or authorize any operational change.

Priority labels describe impact on official-record integrity and intended organizational/cloud or live-billing use. They are not a claim that every future capability is necessary for today's personal Windows workflow. Current-state statements must be rechecked when a prompt is executed.

Suggested sequencing
- Assessment review (4) supplies the approved version foundation for PCP (5).
- Payer configuration (2), utilization (6) and multi-line/remittance work (7) share billing boundaries; coordinate their schema and contracts.
- Retention/holds (3) must precede destructive automated retention.
- Identity (8) and explicit relationship authority (11) underpin broader organizational use.
- Mobile (10) is staged; EVV requires approved applicable policy and interface requirements.
- Workers (15), alerts (16) and restore verification (17) have separate acceptance evidence.
- Sandbox activation (18) requires a reset pause and guard; preservation/cutover (19) is required before resets resume.
- Coordinate shared files, migrations and release documentation when using separate agents or branches. Preserve ongoing work.
