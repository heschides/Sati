# Clinical records, forms and review

**Status:** current architecture reference, October 8, 2026. Detailed rules remain with the linked canonical owners.

Implemented: shared NoteWorkflow/ServiceTimeline/compliance owners, expected revisions, target-identified annual obligations, explicit actual-date attestations and immutable approved-note amendment/assessment submission-review evidence. Approval alone does not attest a form or provide a signature. Immutable review history blocks consumer deletion.

Activation and external policy acceptance remain governed by the clinical runbooks/readiness registry; legacy missing dates or historical submissions must not acquire invented evidence.
