## 2026-09-29 — Modern earth tones darken the surrounding space

The approved Modern Stone preview keeps Modern's typography, flat white reading
surfaces, and clay accent while using a soft gray workspace and a deeper gray
navigation bar. Modern Sand, Taupe, Sage, and Clay apply the same treatment in muted
earth tones. Each is a complete interchangeable theme dictionary offered through
the existing per-user theme picker. The default and existing palettes stay intact.
Functional status colors retain their meaning and must remain legible on the new
surfaces under the existing contrast and rendered-view checks.

Settings presents the theme catalog in style groups, alphabetized within each
group. The earth-tone Modern variants have their own adjacent group. Grouping is
a view concern; existing resource names and per-user saved preferences are unchanged.
