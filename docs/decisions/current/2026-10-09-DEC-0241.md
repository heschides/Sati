## Choice

Adopt the [W8 polling design](../../../BACKGROUND_WORKERS_HANDOFF.md#october-9--polling-fairness-design--task-1331):
agency-first, account-second, Status/ERA feed rotation with separate durable scheduling pivots;
one status page or one oldest ERA artifact per feed offer; at most 100 distinct feed offers per
existing hosted pass. Preserve the five-minute delay after the pass and the shared reset,
poller and vendor request leases. Retain complete bounded ERA listing discovery before choosing
the oldest artifact. A scheduling offer never advances a response checkpoint.

Persistence owns three additive scheduling entities, scoped revision guards, indexes and source
migration. The API owns constructor-injected selection inside a short, zero-retry SQL transaction
under reset and poller admission. Dispose selection before vendor/receipt work. Advance position
before processing so a failed feed spends its offer. Keep receipt/cursor atomicity, retained
account binding, current billing effects, cooperative HTTP deadline and no uncertain upload replay.

Bounded source work is split into task 1.3.3.2 (ERA quantum) and task 1.3.3.3 (durable selector,
bounded discovery/pass, synthetic acceptance). Generate schema source and use newly owned synthetic
fixtures only. Existing databases, controlled migration apply/rollback, reset baseline uptake,
all-host rollout and activation require their separate operational authority and evidence.

## Alternatives and limits

Full account/ERA draining allows an early account to consume most of a pass. Flat account rotation
allocates more offers to agencies with more accounts. Process-only pivots lose progress on restart
or host replacement. Reusing receipt checkpoints, dispatch pivots or preflight readiness would
mix independent safety and scheduling owners. Dedicated agency workers or narrower vendor locks
cannot be justified without quota/capacity evidence. These alternatives are rejected for this slice.

Implementation review confirms the preserved filtered uniqueness rule allows one enabled test
Claim.MD account per agency. Current K ≤ 1; supported replacement is tested. The original
many-active-account acceptance assumption was corrected without relaxing the business constraint.

The accepted bound is conditional scheduling opportunity, not elapsed time, throughput, measured
capacity or aggregate resource isolation. Admission, SQL, key wrapping, parsing and cleanup remain
separate unbounded components. Three tables grow with participating agencies/accounts; this is
durable metadata, not a demonstrated storage quota. Resource inventory 1.4.1 remains assigned to
the second thread. D1–D4 and all operating gates remain authoritative.
