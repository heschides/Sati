## Choice and reason

Adopt [W8's signature design](../../../BACKGROUND_WORKERS_HANDOFF.md#october-10--signature-fairness-design--task-1335):
preserve three ordered phases, ten offers per phase and ten-second first-tick cadence. Use durable
agency-first and in-agency record-ID rotation per phase, with bounded visited exclusions and
committed offers separate from business/receipt evidence. A damaged head or restarted process
cannot automatically reset this scheduling position. Mail eligibility retains due/lease checks;
eligible-ID rotation explicitly replaces global due-time priority, without changing attempt,
suppression, revocation, stable operation GUID or GET-only recovery rules.

First implement/test shared Demo reset participation, injected hosted timing/pass seam and
matching target/gate checks. Then add independent Persistence scheduling metadata/index/source
migration and short zero-retry API selector, share existing projection eligibility, and add
conditional scoped mail processing. Each item keeps reset through authoritative effects/cleanup;
selection disposes before dependencies. Preserve immutable packages, clinical projection,
mail revocation transaction span and uncertain external outcome handling.

Existing process/global scans and repeating failed projection heads do not supply durable agency
opportunities. Flat record rotation favors larger agency backlogs. Parallel phases, immediate
retry, transaction shortening and provider quotas would require different safety/capacity proof.
The selected direction adds shared writes and agency/phase metadata; it is not a storage cap.

## Limits and dependencies

Conditional phase offers are distinct from time, throughput, aggregate capacity or delivery.
Blob/key/render/sender/SQL time, purpose reservations and note restart/membership gaps remain.
DEC-0242 remains proposed; no resource backend/value is adopted. Reset exclusion prevents a
cooperating concurrent reset during a held callback; it does not prove retention/reconciliation
after a later baseline restore of externally processed signature/mail data. That operating guard,
schema apply/baseline/all-host compatibility and activation need separate evidence/authority.
