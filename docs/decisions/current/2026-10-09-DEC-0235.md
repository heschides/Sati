## Choice and reason

Accept one durable account readiness owner separate from dispatch delivery evidence. Typed
missing keys before Sending defer all queued work for that account while preserving intent and
bytes. Contracts owns four delays (1/5/15/60 minutes plus deterministic 0–10% delay), fifth-failure
hold and reopening eligibility. Successful due preflight resumes ordinary current release checks;
explicit same-agency Admin recovery requires restored key/receipt protection and current actor,
binding and revisions. SQL session account exclusion precedes common admission and ends before
upload. Shared/unknown faults and cancellation do not become account failures.

The [W8 owner](../../../BACKGROUND_WORKERS_HANDOFF.md#october-9--known-unsent-dispatch-isolation-implementation)
owns the exact boundary, safe API/UI, source migration/rollback and activation prerequisites.
[Working evidence](../../readiness/work-evidence.md#2026-10-09--missing-key-dispatch-isolation-implementation)
records actual checks and limits. Reject cancelling temporarily blocked due work, process-memory
skip lists, per-dispatch-only failure state, catch-all fault classification, implicit reopen on
configuration/queue change, SQL transactions over key I/O and active mixed worker versions.
