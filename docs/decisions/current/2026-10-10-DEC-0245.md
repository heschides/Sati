## Proposed contract and proof boundary

[W8 protocol](../../../BACKGROUND_WORKERS_HANDOFF.md#october-10--durable-controller-protocol-proposal--task-154)
specifies one durable target/profile/epoch authority, atomic compare-and-commit acknowledgments,
bounded owner slots with generations and per-owner request sequences. Contiguous terminal
sequence retirement retains a high-water rejection floor, so delayed old calls cannot recreate
grants after individual fingerprints are removed. Live/uncertain holes block retirement; clocks
never release debt. Owner-slot reuse and restored/changed epochs need positive external fencing
and closure proof, which controller generation numbers alone cannot supply.

The proposed 16 identity slots are bounded bookkeeping, **not** fixed per-host credit shares;
all hosts still share DEC-0244's proposed protected classes. The candidate has no durable backend
or owner fencing. A separate independently budgeted durable service is the proposed backend
class, with exact atomicity/durability/failure requirements; no vendor/SDK/storage service is
selected or provisioned. Another database on the same protected capacity is not independently
budgeted merely because its name differs.

Task 1.5.5 may test terminal retirement and response-loss/restart semantics with an injected
synthetic state-store seam. Persistent storage, provider rollback, real process death, SQL fencing,
enrollment and bounded transport remain later acceptance. This record neither adopts the proposal
for operation nor changes current SQL/record/financial/signature safety ownership.
