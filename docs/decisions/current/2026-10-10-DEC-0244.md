## Direction and local candidate

[W8](../../../BACKGROUND_WORKERS_HANDOFF.md#october-10--overlapping-host-budget-direction--task-152)
owns the profile and acceptance rationale. Overlapping process incarnations need one authoritative,
strongly consistent controller rather than multiplying per-host limits. Fixed process allocations
are not the first candidate here: no enforceable exclusive slot ownership exists in current source.
Reject the business SQL pool as an uncharged controller: connecting to wait consumes the protected
resource. A concrete external durable controller/backend is not yet selected.

First test an unregistered accounting candidate with one atomic state owner, server-owned costs,
protected class/agency shares and exact target/epoch/request/incarnation identity. Failed or
uncertain cleanup retains the full reservation; neither time passage nor a replacement host
reclaims it. Bounded retained replay records stop grants when full; safe epoch/retirement and fenced
reconciliation need later protocol proofs. No TTL, process lease or controller recovery proves
old SQL connections stopped.

Starting synthetic envelope: 32 logical connection credits (daily 12/recovery 8/background 8/
validation 4), scoped agency cap 4 per class, zero resource-admission waiters, 128 retained records
and 16 child identities per grant. These are test parameters, not pool maxima, deployed quotas or
measured capacity. Try-admission avoids parked SQL; denial cannot create business effects or erase
durable due work. A two-second ordinary casework response goal is proposed for later representative
load acceptance, not guaranteed by the accounting tests.

No current route, worker or database uses the candidate. Registered-route ordering, all consumers/
physical pools, durable-controller outage/restart/disaster state, owner fencing, dependency/CPU
limits and load targets remain integration/activation prerequisites. One shared in-memory model
proves its transitions, not distributed consistency or provider failure. Actual backend adoption
requires a concrete reviewed successor and actual-call evidence.
