## Context and choice

`NoteAbandonmentWorker.RunDueAsync` formerly materialized every ordered agency ID before its
loop. The existing 100-note sweep bound limited one agency's candidates, not that discovery list.
With 251 stable synthetic agencies, a reader observer saw all 251 IDs in one query.

Use the named `AgencyDiscoveryPageSize` of 100. Capture `MaxAsync` over nullable agency IDs,
then traverse ordered keyset pages at or below that maximum. A nullable initial cursor applies
no first-page lower bound, preserving fail-closed handling of damaged nonpositive agency IDs.
Advance the cursor to every page's last observed ID, including completed and failed agencies.
Keep the distinct `NoteAbandonmentSweep.WorkerBatchSize` of 100 notes.

When the pass could otherwise mark the day complete, use a bounded `AnyAsync` for IDs above
the maximum; for an initially empty range, check for any agency. Observed growth keeps the day
due so the next existing hourly pass can visit it while skipping already completed agencies.
If a fault or at-limit result already keeps the day due, no additional growth query is needed.
Maximum/page/growth failures remain shared failures outside the agency-sweep recoverable catch.

Preserve the global SQL/reset leases, cancellation/disablement checks, DEC-0222 fault policy,
atomic note/audit transaction and committed-count rules, successful-agency/day skipping,
next-local-day catch-up and default-off/hourly/no-idle-SQL cadence. Change no route, schema,
authoritative business rule, provider quota, dispatch/account scheduling or worker activation.

## Alternatives and consequences

An unbounded list retains the original materialization risk. Offset paging does not provide
the explicit key progression used here. A zero initial cursor silently omits damaged nonpositive
IDs. Discovery without a captured upper bound can chase newly growing higher IDs indefinitely.
Freezing membership in memory recreates the unbounded list; introducing a durable membership
snapshot or transaction isolation design would enlarge this local slice.

The chosen upper bound is a finite key range, not a frozen membership snapshot. Agencies can
appear or disappear between reads. Within-range inserts after the last discovery query can be
missed even above the last cursor, as can lower-ID inserts/reseeding behind it and growth after
the final existence check. Complete provisioning/membership invalidation remains deferred.
Do not claim visitation of every start-time or newly provisioned agency.

This bounds each agency-ID materialization to 100; it does not bound successful-day dictionary
cardinality, total turns/time, lock duration or healthy-agency wait. Stale-local-day cache retirement
is the next eligible slice. Full fairness, total budgets, aggregate admission/capacity and live
progress evidence retain their W8 owners and separate acceptance.
