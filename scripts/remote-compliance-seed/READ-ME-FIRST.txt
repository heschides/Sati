Clearing old overdue items from Sati
====================================

Your Sati database is not the record of your work; your tracking sheet, Evergreen,
and Credible are. This tool makes Sati agree with them: every compliance item that
is already past due is recorded as done ON ITS OWN DUE DATE. Anything due today or
later is left alone, so Sati can track what is coming due from here on.

What it records, all due before today:
  - PCPs, assessments, Reclasses, Safety Plans, Privacy Practices, and 90-day reviews
  - DHHS, agency, and medical releases, including earlier years Sati never listed
  - an opened date for any PCP or assessment past its open/start date
  - any annual form rows Sati has not created yet (it would add them on opening)

Every one of these is marked "Seeded ... to match the external tracking sheet; not a
record of completion", so they can always be told apart from real entries. It never
creates or changes notes, clients, or scratchpad entries. The client list's "Last
contact" line still reflects only the visits, calls, and emails you have entered.

WHAT TO DO
----------
1. Copy this whole folder to your Desktop. Running it from the shared folder can be
   blocked.

2. Double-click "Step 1 - CHECK ONLY". It changes nothing. It shows counts (no names)
   and saves Sati-compliance-seed-check.txt to your Desktop. Note TOTAL CHANGES.

   "Left alone, needs a look" lists items it will not touch because Sati's own rules
   refuse the date, for example a Reclass whose assessment was actually finished
   later. Those stay overdue; fix them by hand in Sati.

3. Close Sati. Double-click "Step 2 - APPLY", type the TOTAL CHANGES number and YES.
   It backs up your database, rehearses on a copy, then applies. It saves
   Sati-compliance-seed-apply.txt to your Desktop.

4. If it ends with DONE, start Sati. Otherwise send the file to Josh; your database
   is unchanged, and the backup it names holds the database as it was.
